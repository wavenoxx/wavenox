# Step 7 project lifecycle

An enquiry remains a consultation. Only an owner may explicitly activate a project from a current `contract_signed` consultation, supplying separate contact verification and commercial/operational authorization evidence. That status alone is not proof of an executed agreement. A unique consultation key prevents duplicate projects; original notes, consent and attribution remain unchanged.

## Milestones

1. Project confirmed
2. Site survey
3. Engineering review
4. DISCOM / net-metering coordination
5. Equipment preparation
6. Installation scheduled
7. Installation underway
8. Testing & commissioning
9. Handover
10. Aftercare & support

Staff record not_started, in_progress, blocked, completed or not_applicable. Starting work precedes completion; blocked work must return to in_progress. Completed work can be corrected back to in_progress with an attributable reason. Non-applicable work can be reopened. No date or percentage fabricates progress. Displayed counts are counts of explicit milestone states, not installed capacity or measured energy.

Commissioning and handover cannot be marked not_applicable. Completion requires a reviewed same-project document of the corresponding category and the preceding operational milestone. Installation cannot be reversed while commissioning remains complete; commissioning cannot be reversed while handover remains complete. Evidence cannot be withdrawn or replaced while a completed milestone depends on it. Owner project completion requires both critical milestones and their evidence. Completed/cancelled projects must be reopened before milestone/coordinator changes. Staff remain accountable for the truth of submitted evidence; software cannot independently certify engineering or legal validity.

## Appointments

Customers may create requested appointments only. Staff propose, confirm, reschedule, cancel and record actual completion. Requested/proposed/rescheduled is not confirmed. UTC timestamps are displayed in Asia/Kolkata; intervals must be positive and at most eight hours. Confirmed appointments cannot overlap for the same modeled coordinator, including across projects. Completion requires an existing confirmed visit that has ended and unchanged scheduled timestamps. Closed appointments cannot be edited. No calendar integration or automated dispatch is implied.

## Documents, access and aftercare

Scans are private and pending until reviewed. Internal is the default classification. Customer visibility requires both explicit customer classification and verified status. Replacement creates a new immutable object/version; superseded files cannot be downloaded through the application. Review means staff approval for the declared purpose, not cryptographic signature verification or malware certification.

Invited customers must verify their approved email, and may access multiple explicitly associated projects. They can read published updates, view appointments, download authorized scans and submit support. They cannot change milestones, approve handover, assign staff or edit project identity. Owners revoke project membership separately from retained operational records. Aftercare is supported through explicit milestones, updates and support responses; no inverter monitoring is present.
