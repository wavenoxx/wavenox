## 1. Executive Summary

Workstream 1B transforms WAVENOX's 11 duplicated, mismatched, or abstract CGI media assets into a cohesive, original, 15-slug collection of architectural solar photography while preserving the 4 highest-scoring existing hero assets (`home-hero`, `res-hero`, `omnigrid-hero`, `commercial-hero`).

Every primary media slug in `src/config/media.ts` now resolves to a **unique, non-duplicated SHA-256 hash** (`15` unique primary slugs + `1` backward-compatibility alias `homes-hero -> res-hero`; `0` duplicate primary slugs, down from `3` duplicate pairs in Phase 0), with **1:1 matching desktop (`16:9`) and mobile (`9:16`) scene continuity** and **zero physical-to-`srcset` dimension mismatches**.

## 2. Image-Generation Capability & Production Workflow

1. **Primary Image Generation Tool:** Built-in Antigravity `image-generator` subagent (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`, native `1376×768` for `16:9` widescreen masters and `768×1376` for `9:16` portrait masters).
2. **Post-Processing & Derivative Pipeline:** Node.js `sharp` (`Lanczos3` downsampling, `withoutEnlargement: true`, MozJPEG, WebP `smartSubsample`, AVIF `effort: 6`, and pixel-level luminance/color normalization via `scripts/build-images.mjs`).
3. **Quota & Provenance Transparency:**
   - Across two `image-generator` (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`) batches before hitting the model's 5-hour rolling quota cap (`429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`), 6 brand-new `1376×768` (`16:9`) architectural masters were generated specifically for Workstream 1B (`p1b_home_design_v1_1794588721763.jpg`, `p1b_res_terrace_v1_1794588750372.jpg`, `p1b_home_outage_v1_1794588784481.jpg`, `p1b_omnigrid_switchover_v1_1794848707296.jpg`, `p1b_commercial_industrial_v1_1794848757139.jpg`, and `p1b_commercial_industrial_v1_1794848729602.jpg`).
   - Five additional original `1376×768` (`16:9`) and `768×1376` (`9:16`) WAVENOX masters previously generated by `gemini-3.1-flash-image` in this conversation's artifact storage—and never duplicated across active slugs—were curated and color-graded in Sharp for `omnigrid-night` (`home_hero_villa_1790249603098.jpg` + `home_hero_mobile_1790270808850.jpg`), `res-weather` (`home_design_macro_1790249666360.jpg` + `home_design_mobile_1790270939798.jpg`), `home-heat` (`liquid_glass_macro_1790184547514.jpg` with neon-orange underglow neutralized), `home-final` (`home_hero_v3_1790437152784.jpg`), and `studio-estate` (`home_hero_v4_1790437185079.jpg`).
