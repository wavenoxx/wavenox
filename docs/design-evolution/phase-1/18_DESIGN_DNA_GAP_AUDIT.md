## 2. Ranked Architectural Design Gaps (Impact-Ordered)

### Priority 1 (`P1`) — Major Visual Composition & Subject Visibility Defects

1. **Scene 03 (`#design` + `#heat-performance`) — Full-Bleed Dark Scrim Obscuring Architectural Detail + Mobile Disclaimer Clipping**
   - **Baseline Observation:** Rendered as a second full-viewport dark `<Panel>` (`min-h-[100svh]`, `overflow-hidden`) with top/bottom dark gradients (`rgba(11,14,19,0.62)` to `0.88`), a floating pill badge (`Architecture & Thermal Engineering`), 3 overlaid stats, 2 buttons, and a 2-line footnote. On mobile (`390×844`), the bottom line of the reference footnote (`specs follow site survey and OEM datasheet.`) is physically clipped by the section bottom boundary.
   - **Design Principle Violated:** Not every architectural photograph should be used as a darkened wallpaper behind white text. Hardware craftsmanship, concealed conduit raceways, and sandstone terrace integration require an unobstructed **Pattern B — Large Editorial Architectural Frame**.
   - **Correction:** Convert `#design` into an alabaster/white editorial showcase (`max-w-[1240px]`) with a 12-column split header (`#heat-performance` eyebrow + H2 left, concise explanation + `SpecsDrawer` trigger + `/residential` link right), a large un-darkened `16:9` inset architectural photograph (`media["home-design"]`), and a 3-column hairline specification ledger (`-0.30% / °C`, `22.45%+`, `158 km/h`) with unclipped disclosures below.

2. **Scene 04A (`#outage-protection`) — Lead Copy Colliding with Rooftop Solar Array & Mobile Footnote Clipping**
   - **Baseline Observation:** On desktop (`1440×900`), `pt-24` (`96px`) top padding combined with a 2-line H2 and 3-line `54ch` lead pushes the white paragraph (`"Standard grid-tied solar shuts down during power cuts..."`) directly across the twilight villa's rooftop solar array. On mobile (`390×844`), the 2 stacked full-width CTA buttons (`100px` combined height) push the 3-line disclaimer out the bottom of `overflow-hidden`, slicing the final line in half.
   - **Design Principle Violated:** Text must occupy natural negative space (the upper twilight sky) and never collide with the solar array or clip at viewport edges.
   - **Correction:** Refine non-hero `<Panel>` top spacing (`pt-12 sm:pt-14 lg:pt-16`), widen desktop H2 (`max-w-[28ch]`) and lead (`max-w-[64ch]`) so the entire header block stays in the upper sky above the roofline, open the middle focal band (`min-h-[160px] sm:min-h-[220px] lg:min-h-[260px]`), and use a responsive 2-column CTA grid (`min-[375px]:grid-cols-2`) on mobile so both buttons sit side-by-side in a single `44px` row—freeing `56px` of vertical height and eliminating mobile footnote clipping.

3. **Scene 05 (`#savings`) — Gray-on-White-on-Gray Box Nesting & Dashboard Clutter**
   - **Baseline Observation:** Inside a `#F4F4F4` section sits a white card (`max-w-3xl`), and inside that white card sit 4 nested `#F4F4F4` boxes (3 metric boxes + 1 generation summary pill) plus a `#F4F4F4` select control and `#F4F4F4` button. On mobile (`390×844`), the 3 stacked gray metric boxes make the calculator unnecessarily tall and visually repetitive.
   - **Design Principle Violated:** Premium financial interfaces use typographic scale, tabular numerals, and `1px` architectural hairlines (`#E3E4E6`) rather than nested gray cards inside white cards.
   - **Correction:** Widen the financial sheet (`max-w-[1080px]`), arrange DISCOM selection and the Monthly Electricity Bill slider in a balanced 12-column top split, replace the 3 nested gray boxes with hairline-divided architectural columns (`30px–40px` medium tabular numerals), and align the generation summary alongside the Design Studio and Advisor CTAs.

### Priority 2 (`P2`) — Editorial Cohesion, Spatial Rhythm & Component Polish

4. **Scene 07 (`#faq`) — Boxed Card-in-Section Accordion Treatment**
   - **Baseline Observation:** Places the `<Faq>` accordion inside a narrow centered white box (`max-w-3xl bg-[#FFFFFF] rounded-[8px] border`) below the 3 governance columns.
   - **Correction:** Present the 3 verifiable governance foundations (`Three-Tier Warranty Clarity`, `Transparent Tariff & Subsidy Basis`, `Coordinated Delivery & Privacy`) with crisp top hairlines (`border-t border-[#D8DADC] pt-6`), followed by a 12-column editorial split (4-column Knowledge Base header + direct links on the left; 8-column borderless `<Faq>` accordion on the right).

5. **Scene 04B (`#monitoring`) — Floating Thick-Bezel Phone Mockup**
   - **Baseline Observation:** Right column floats a thick `rounded-[32px] border-4 border-[#393C41]` phone frame directly on white space without an architectural stage.
   - **Correction:** Frame the dark OEM telemetry interface inside a calm `#F4F4F4` editorial stage (`rounded-[6px] border border-[#E3E4E6] p-6 sm:p-10 lg:p-12`) paired with a structured left-hand narrative column and a 2-row dispatch/switchover specification key.

6. **Scene 08 (`#consultation`) & `<Footer />` — Duplicate Back-to-Back Conversion Buttons**
   - **Baseline Observation:** `#consultation` renders two primary conversion buttons (`Get Solar Estimate` and `Request Consultation`), and `<Footer />` immediately repeats two more conversion buttons (`Design Your System` and `Talk to a Solar Advisor`) 100px below in its top strip.
   - **Correction:** Refine `#consultation` top spacing so its headline and subtitle sit cleanly in the twilight sky above the courtyard residence roofline, and quiet the `<Footer />` top identity strip into a restrained brand + domain scope header (`Residential · Battery Storage · Commercial Solar`) without redundant buttons.
