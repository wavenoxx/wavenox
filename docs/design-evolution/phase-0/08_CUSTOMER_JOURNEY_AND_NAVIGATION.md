## 1. Customer Simplicity Test — Three Representative User Journeys

Our guiding principle:

> **A customer should not need to understand solar engineering jargon to take the next step.**  
> Detailed technical specifications (`N-Type TOPCon`, `-0.30%/°C`, `TGERC Clause 8.1.3`) must remain one click away when requested, without blocking a first-time visitor.

---

## 2. Navigation & Information Architecture Blueprint

### 2.1 Evaluation of Current Navigation (`Header.tsx`, `MegaMenu.tsx`, `Footer.tsx`)

1. **Desktop Top Bar (`Header.tsx`):** The 5-item center rail (`Solar Panels`, `Solar + Storage`, `Commercial`, `Design Studio`, `Discover`) + right Region Globe trigger + `Talk to Advisor` CTA is clean, balanced, and matches our `56px` showroom standard. **Verdict: KEEP.**
2. **Desktop `<MegaMenu>` (`MegaMenu.tsx`):**
   - _Current Issue:_ Hovering `Solar Panels` (`EV-WVX-07`) renders 3 visual cards (each containing 2 separate text links: `Learn` and `Get Estimate`, where all three `Get Estimate` links point to `/deploy`) plus 7 right-rail links = **13 links inside a single hover panel**.
   - _Blueprint Refinement:_ Make each visual card in `<MegaMenu>` a **single unified clickable `<Link>`** (clicking anywhere on the thumbnail, title, or subtitle navigates to its destination, removing the repetitive `Learn · Get Estimate` sub-links under every card) and limit the right-rail list to **5 distinct, high-value links** per category.
3. **Mobile Slide-Over Drawer (`Header.tsx`):**
   - The 2-level drill-down sheet (`EV-WVX-08`) with `48px` touch rows, `< All Categories` back button, and bottom `Get Solar Estimate` + `Talk to a Solar Advisor` buttons is ergonomic and overflow-free at `320px` and `390px`. **Verdict: KEEP.**
4. **Strict Separation of Public vs. Private Operational Routes:**
   - Public navigation (`Header`, `MegaMenu`, `Footer`) must **never** expose staff CRM routes (`/crm`, `/crm/login`) or private customer portal operational tools unless explicitly accessed by authorized users. `/portal` remains a discreet customer support/tracking utility, and `/crm` remains completely unindexed (`robots: noindex, nofollow`) and omitted from public navigation.

## 3. Conversion, Regulatory & Trust Safeguards (Non-Negotiable Rules)

Every Phase 1 visual refinement must preserve the following verified engineering and legal safeguards established across Steps 1–8:

| Safeguard Domain                            | Verified Existing Implementation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Phase 1 Preservation Mandate                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Explicit Customer Consent**            | `ConsultationDrawer.tsx` and `/deploy` Consultation Modal require explicit user interaction with the consent checkbox (`consentGiven: z.literal(true)`) before submission, citing `/privacy` (DPDP Act 2023).                                                                                                                         | **MUST PRESERVE 100%.** Never pre-check or hide the consent checkbox.                                                                         |
| **2. Source & Campaign Attribution**        | `src/utils/attribution.ts` captures first-touch and last-touch UTM parameters, referrer, landing path, and triggering CTA `source` (`home-hero`, `header-desktop`, `deploy-studio`, etc.).                                                                                                                                            | **MUST PRESERVE 100%.** Every refined CTA button opening `<ConsultationDrawer>` must pass an accurate `source` identifier.                    |
| **3. `LIVE` vs. `DEMO` Mode Separation**    | `submitConsultationServerFn` (`src/server/consultation.ts`) clearly distinguishes `mode: "LIVE"` (persisted to Supabase `consultations` table with reference ID) from `mode: "DEMO"` (safe preview validation when Supabase service credentials are not configured).                                                                  | **MUST PRESERVE 100%.** Success screens must display the exact server-returned `mode` and reference code without faking database persistence. |
| **4. Design Studio Handoff (`/deploy`)**    | Homepage `#savings` passes `?bill=${monthlyBill}&discom=${selectedDiscom}` directly into `/deploy`, pre-populating Step 1 of the 6-step studio.                                                                                                                                                                                       | **MUST PRESERVE 100%.** Scene 5 must maintain the exact `?bill=&discom=` URL query contract.                                                  |
| **5. Indicative vs. Binding Offer Clarity** | All financial estimates clearly state they are preliminary engineering estimates subject to physical 3D sun-path shading analysis, sanctioned load verification, and formal contract execution.                                                                                                                                       | **MUST PRESERVE 100%.** Never style indicative savings previews to look like binding invoices or guaranteed utility payouts.                  |
| **6. Indian Tariff & Subsidy Accuracy**     | Telangana uses verified TGERC Proceeding No. TGERC/Secy/77/2025 dated 29.12.2025 (`₹2.57/kWh` export settlement, Clause 8.1.3); Andhra Pradesh uses verified APERC telescopic slabs (`₹2.09/kWh` indicative export benchmark); PM Surya Ghar `₹78,000` DBT cap applies strictly to eligible residential grid-connected rooftop solar. | **MUST PRESERVE 100%.** Zero US-specific ITC tax credits, net-billing myths, or unverified tariff numbers may ever be introduced.             |
| **7. Zero Fabricated Social Proof**         | `scripts/copy-lint.mjs` enforces zero fake customer reviews, zero unverified installation counts, and zero false manufacturer claims.                                                                                                                                                                                                 | **MUST PRESERVE 100%.** Run `npm run lint:copy` (`scripts/copy-lint.mjs`) as a mandatory gate on all Phase 1 edits.                           |
