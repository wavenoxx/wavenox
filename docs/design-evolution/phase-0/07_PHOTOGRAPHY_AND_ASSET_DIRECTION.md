## 2. Image Pipeline & Hero LCP Optimization Plan (`scripts/build-images.mjs`)

1. **Prevent Upscaling (`withoutEnlargement: true`):**
   - Currently, `scripts/build-images.mjs` runs `.resize({ width })` for `[640, 960, 1280, 1600, 1920]` without `withoutEnlargement: true`, stretching `1024px`, `1200px`, and `1376px` masters up to `1920px` and inflating byte size with blurry interpolated pixels.
   - **Phase 1 Rule:** Add `withoutEnlargement: true` to all Sharp `.resize()` calls and ensure all new full-bleed masters are authored at `>= 2400px` wide (`16:9` desktop) and `>= 1440px` wide (`9:16` mobile).
2. **Fix `<link rel="preload">` Codec Mismatch (Eliminates Double Hero Download):**
   - Currently, `src/routes/index.tsx`, `residential.tsx`, `omnigrid.tsx`, and `enterprise.tsx` preload `.webp` (`type="image/webp"`), while `<Media>` places `<source type="image/avif">` first—causing Chromium/WebKit browsers to download **both** the `.webp` preload AND the `.avif` picture source.
   - **Phase 1 Rule:** Update `<head>` preload links to `type="image/avif"` with matching `media="(max-width: 767px)"` and `media="(min-width: 768px)"` `imageSrcSet` attributes.
3. **Mobile Hero Byte Budget (`< 65 KB` for `home-hero-mobile.avif`):**
   - `public/media/home-hero-mobile.avif` is currently `436.2 KB` (`800px` wide), whereas `home-hero-mobile.webp` is `181.6 KB`.
   - **Phase 1 Rule:** Resize `home-hero-mobile` to `720w` (`360px` `@2x`) and encode AVIF at `quality: 50, effort: 6` (target `45 KB–65 KB`) and WebP at `quality: 74` (target `60 KB–80 KB`), cutting `/` mobile transfer weight by over `500 KB`.
