## 1. Classification Taxonomy

Every figure and parameter in the WAVENOX calculation engine is tagged with both a **`DataCategory`** and a **`VerificationStatus`**, and each `EstimateResult` exposes a granular **`verificationBreakdown`**:

- **DataCategory:**
  - `OFFICIAL_REGULATORY_FACT`: Statutory orders, Gazette rules, or official SERC/MNRE notifications.
  - `ENGINEERING_MODEL_ASSUMPTION`: Industry-standard solar engineering coefficients (NASA POWER 22-year climatology + NREL PVWatts v8 `14.08%` system loss derate, module area, degradation).
  - `COMMERCIAL_PRICING_ASSUMPTION`: Published public-sector bank loan schedules (conditional on borrower credit appraisal and EBLR spreads) or indicative turnkey capex and corporate tax shield models.

- **VerificationStatus:**
  - `VERIFIED`: Exact statutory/SERC formula or schedule implemented and verified against primary regulatory records.
  - `INDICATIVE`: Regional blended benchmark, engineering simulation output, or conditional bank financing rate that requires site/bill/lender confirmation.
  - `REQUIRES_VERIFICATION`: Conditional eligibility requiring consumer/DISCOM/portal verification.
  - `UNAVAILABLE`: Unsupported or unrecognized utility/parameter; engine withholds fabricated financial figures (`0`) and surfaces an explicit warning.

- **Granular `verificationBreakdown` on `estimate()` (`P1-E`):**
  - `tariffSchedule`: `VERIFIED` for Telangana (`TGSPDCL`, `TGNPDCL`) and Andhra Pradesh (`APEPDCL`, `APSPDCL`); `INDICATIVE` for 7 other listed regional utilities; `UNAVAILABLE` for unknown DISCOMs.
  - `solarGeneration`: `INDICATIVE` for all 7 modeled service corridors (raw NASA POWER GHI/temperature is verified, while simulated kWh/kWp/yr AC output with `14.08%` PVWatts loss derate is an engineering model assumption); `UNAVAILABLE` for unknown DISCOMs.
  - `subsidyEligibility`: `POTENTIALLY_ELIGIBLE`, `CONFIRMED_ELIGIBLE`, `REQUIRES_VERIFICATION`, or `INELIGIBLE`.
  - `financing`: `INDICATIVE` for all supported utilities (subject to borrower underwriting and floating EBLR); `UNAVAILABLE` for unknown DISCOMs.
  - `completeEstimate`: `INDICATIVE` for supported utilities (since multi-year savings, generation, and financing depend on site and customer conditions); `UNAVAILABLE` for unknown DISCOMs.
