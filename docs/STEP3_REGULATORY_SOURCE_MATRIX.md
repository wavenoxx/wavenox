## 1. Classification Taxonomy

Every figure and parameter in the WAVENOX calculation engine is tagged with both a **`DataCategory`** and a **`VerificationStatus`**:

- **DataCategory:**
  - `OFFICIAL_REGULATORY_FACT`: Statutory orders, Gazette rules, or official SERC/MNRE notifications.
  - `ENGINEERING_MODEL_ASSUMPTION`: Industry-standard solar engineering coefficients (PVGIS TMY irradiation, PR/derate factors, module area, degradation).
  - `COMMERCIAL_LENDER_ASSUMPTION`: Published public-sector bank terms (subject to bank sanction and credit appraisal) or indicative benchmark capex/tax shields.

- **VerificationStatus:**
  - `VERIFIED`: Exact statutory/SERC formula or schedule implemented and verified against primary regulatory records.
  - `INDICATIVE`: Regional blended benchmark or preliminary sizing estimate that requires site/bill confirmation.
  - `REQUIRES_VERIFICATION`: Conditional eligibility requiring consumer/DISCOM/portal verification.
  - `UNAVAILABLE`: Unsupported or unrecognized utility/parameter; engine degrades safely without fabricated math.
